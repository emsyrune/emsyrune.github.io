Pastloop privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Pastloop is a puzzle game for iPhone and Android published by Emsy Rune. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • There is no account to create, and we do not ask for your email or other contact details.
  • The game keeps your best score, aids and settings on your phone.
  • The global leaderboard is optional: only if you choose "Join" are your Game Center (iPhone) or Play Games (Android) name and your best score saved in our database (Google Firebase) and shown to all players. You can leave whenever you like.
  • The app is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob: rewarded videos, which play only when you choose to watch them, and a banner on the start screen and after a game (never during play). In Europe, personalised ads appear only if you consent.

Data controller

Marco Vita, who publishes apps under the name Emsy Rune. Contact: emsyrune@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores on your device your best score, the aids you have left (Preview, Freeze, Undo), the game in progress and your settings (sounds, vibration, language). This data stays on your phone and is deleted by uninstalling the app. Only your best score, and only if you join the leaderboard, is also sent to our database (see below).

Global leaderboard (optional)

The leaderboard compares players' best scores across iPhone and Android. To take part you sign in with Game Center (Apple) on iPhone or Play Games (Google) on Android; the game then asks whether you want to join. We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, with the database in Europe):

  • when you sign in, Firebase Authentication creates an identifier linked to your Game Center or Play Games account, together with your name, used to recognise you between launches;
  • only if you choose "Join" do we save in the leaderboard database your Game Center or Play Games name (the public name you chose there) and your best score (the most gems you have taken). If you choose "Not now", your name and best score do not go on the leaderboard.

Your name and best score are visible to all Pastloop players on the leaderboard. We do not store your email, phone number, location or any other data from your Apple or Google account. Sign-in to Game Center and Play Games is handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

To leave the leaderboard, tap Leave the leaderboard on the Leaderboard page: your name and best score are deleted from the database straight away. The sign-in identifier stays in Firebase Authentication until you ask us to delete it by writing to emsyrune@gmail.com. You change your name in the Game Center or Play Games settings, and it is updated on the leaderboard the next time you open the game.

Advertising: Google AdMob

For rewarded videos (to continue after a lost game or to get an aid) and for the banner on the start screen and after a game, the app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for the European Economic Area) and Google LLC. When the app requests an ad, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and operating system information (model, language, version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps) and technical data used to measure them and prevent fraud.

Google uses this data to select and show ads, measure their performance and prevent abuse. With your consent it may also be used for personalised ads, together with Google's advertising partners, which are listed in the privacy message under Manage options.

Learn more: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your consent

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. On first launch Google's privacy message appears, where you can accept, decline or choose in detail. If you decline, the game works exactly the same and videos will be non-personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy & ads button in the game's Settings (the gear on the start screen).
  • iPhone. iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can change this in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Legal bases

Personalised ads and the use of the advertising identifier are based on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (Art. 6(1)(f)). The leaderboard is based on your consent, given by choosing "Join" (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you withdraw by leaving the leaderboard.

Transfers outside the European Union

Google (for AdMob and Firebase) may process data outside the EU, for example in the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).

Retention

Your leaderboard name and best score are kept while you take part and deleted straight away when you leave. The sign-in identifier is kept until you ask us to delete it. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept for the periods stated in its privacy policy.

Children

Pastloop is not specifically aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 14. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw your consent. For leaderboard data you can leave the leaderboard in the app or write to us; for advertising data you can contact Google directly; for any question write to emsyrune@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If the way the app uses data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
