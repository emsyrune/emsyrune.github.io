Too Late privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Too Late is a game for iPhone and Android published by Emsy Rune. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and how you stay in control.

In short

  • We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't ask for your email or any other contact details.
  • The game saves your best score, coins, skins, progress and settings on your phone only. We have no servers collecting game data.
  • The app is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob: rewarded videos, which play only when you choose to watch them, and full-screen ads between runs (never during play, at most one every 4 runs). In Europe, personalised ads appear only if you consent.

Data controller

Marco Vita, who publishes apps as Emsy Rune. Contact: emsyrune@gmail.com.

Data saved on your phone

The app saves on your device your best score, coins, unlocked skins and trails, missions, achievements, game statistics, your best daily-challenge run, your settings (sound, music, vibration, motion) and your consent choice. This data stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Game configuration

At launch the app downloads a small configuration file from this site (emsyrune.github.io, hosted by GitHub) that sets how often ads appear between runs. The request contains no data about you; as with any website, GitHub receives your device's IP address under its privacy statement.

Sharing your result

If you tap Share, the app creates a picture with your distance and delay and hands it to your phone's share menu. You choose whether and with whom to share it; the app doesn't send it anywhere.

Advertising: Google AdMob

For rewarded videos (rewinding after a fall, doubling coins, trying a skin) and for ads between runs, the app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for the European Economic Area) and Google LLC. When the app requests an ad, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and operating system information (model, language, version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps) and technical data to measure them and prevent fraud.

Google uses this data to select and show ads, measure their results and prevent abuse. With your consent it may also be used for personalised ads, together with Google's advertising partners listed in the privacy message under Manage options.

Learn more: how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your consent

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. On first launch, Google's privacy message lets you accept, decline or choose in detail. If you decline, the game works the same and ads are non-personalised. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy and ads button in the game's Settings.
  • iPhone. iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can change your mind in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Legal bases

Personalised ads and the use of the advertising identifier are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Transfers outside the European Union

Google (for AdMob) and GitHub may process data outside the EU, for example in the United States, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).

Retention

Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept for the periods stated in its policy.

Children

Too Late is not specifically aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 14. If you think this has happened, please write to us.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw your consent. We keep no data about you outside your phone; for advertising data you can contact Google directly; for any question write to emsyrune@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If the app changes how it uses data (for example with leaderboards or cloud saves), we'll update this page and the date above before releasing the new version.
