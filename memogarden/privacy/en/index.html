1. Data controller

The controller of your data is Marco Vita, who publishes apps under the name Emsy Rune, and who can be contacted at memogarden.info@gmail.com.

Write to this address with any question about this policy or to exercise your rights.

2. Data we process

  • Email and password (stored encrypted) — to create and manage your account.
  • IP address and technical connection data — recorded in system and authentication logs, for security and diagnostics.
  • GPS position (latitude and longitude) — to tie your memories to a place and show you the ones planted near you.
  • Photo (optional) — if you attach one to a memory, it is uploaded and stored together with the memory.
  • Profile photo (optional) — if you set one, it is checked automatically (see section 4) and uploaded to a public space: anyone with the link can see it, and it appears on your pins and your profile.
  • Hidden photo data (date and place taken) — if the photo you pick contains it, we read it on your device to pre-fill the memory. The photo is recompressed before upload and this data is not sent to our servers.
  • The text of the memories you write.
  • The category and visibility of each memory — private (only you), friends (you and your accepted contacts) or public (anyone physically nearby).
  • The creation date of the memory.
  • Username — chosen by you at sign-up; visible to anyone who sees a memory of yours (based on its visibility) and to anyone searching for you to add you as a friend.
  • Friends — the connections between you and other users: requests sent, received, accepted or removed.
  • Reactions — when you heart a memory, we save the link between you, the memory and the reaction.
  • Comments — the text you write under a memory, visible to anyone who sees that memory.
  • Blocks — if you block another user, we keep the link between you and the blocked user in order to enforce the block.
  • Tags — if you tag a friend in a memory, or a friend tags you, we store the link so it can be shown to anyone who can see that memory.
  • Muted places, notification center and milestones — the places you chose not to get alerts for, the notifications you receive in the app, and the milestones you unlock (for example pin styles).
  • Reports — if you report content, we keep the reported memory, the reason and who made the report, to handle moderation.
  • App usage events (e.g. session opened, memory planted or viewed) — linked to your account and kept for as long as it exists; they stay in our database and are not shared with third-party analytics tools such as Google Analytics or Meta.

We do not process: audio, video, your contact list, payment data.

Optional notifications (Profile → Notifications): you can separately turn on “near a memory”, “reactions to your memories” and “friend requests”. All are off by default.

If you turn any of them on, we store a technical identifier of your device (push token) so we can notify you even when the app is closed. Only “near a memory” also checks your location in the background (subject to the separate “Always” system permission) and stores your last approximate known area, updated only when the local detection updates and not continuously. The other two notifications do not use your location.

Turning a notification off deletes the data specific to that feature from our servers; the push token remains only if you still have at least one other notification on.

The “AI story” of a memory is generated by sending the memory text to Anthropic — see section 4. It is optional: if you don’t request it, or if generation is unavailable, the memory is saved with only the text you wrote.

Photos, memory text and comments are automatically checked before publication by sending them to OpenAI — see section 4. The check is not optional: content that is not allowed is rejected with a message.

Place name and map: to write the place name (“Trastevere, Rome”), the memory’s coordinates are sent to the operating system’s geocoding service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). The map is provided by Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android: to show it, these providers receive the area you are looking at and your IP address, as in any app with a map.

3. Legal bases for processing

We process your data on the following legal bases (Article 6 GDPR):

  • Creating and managing your account, saving and displaying your memories, authentication: performance of the contract with you (the Terms of Service you accept when you sign up).
  • Geolocation, in the foreground and in the background (proximity notifications): your consent, given through the system permissions and the controls in the app, withdrawable at any time.
  • Crash diagnostics, security, abuse prevention and rate limits: our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and safe.
  • Publishing memories, reactions, comments and profile photos visible to other users: your voluntary choice to use these features.
  • Handling reports and blocks: our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe.
  • Automatic content check before publication: our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and unlawful content.
  • Place name and map: performance of the contract, because they are needed for the app’s main function.

Where the basis is consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

4. Who we share data with

We rely on the providers below. Supabase, Anthropic, OpenAI, Sentry and Expo process data as processors on our behalf, each bound by a data processing agreement (DPA); Apple and Google, for system features, maps and notification delivery, act as independent controllers.

  • Supabase — database, authentication, photo storage and server functions. Data is hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Anthropic PBC (United States) — receives only the memory text, and only when you use “Make it magic”, to generate a short narrative version of it. Under the agreement in place, it does not use the transmitted content to train its models and retains it for a limited period for security and abuse prevention. The transfer outside the European Economic Area is based on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission.
  • OpenAI (United States) — receives photos and the text of memories and comments, only for an automatic safety check (moderation) before publication: it is used to block content that is not allowed, not to generate text or images. The check is not optional, because without it we cannot publish the content. The transfer outside the European Economic Area is based on the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission.
  • Sentry (United States) — receives crash and performance diagnostics data: technical device identifiers, operating system and app version, information about the error or response times. We do not link them to your account. The transfer outside the European Economic Area is based on the Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Push notification delivery — when you turn notifications on, the device token and the notification content pass through Expo’s infrastructure and the operating system notification services: Apple Push Notification service on iOS and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android.
  • Apple and Google (independent controllers) — the operating system’s geocoder and maps and, for notifications, the delivery services above. They process data under their own policies: apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

Memories you mark as “public” are visible to any other user physically near that place; “friends” ones only to your accepted contacts who are nearby. This is how the app is meant to work, not a disclosure to outside third parties.

Your username, and the reactions and comments you leave, are visible according to the same visibility rules as the memory they relate to. Your friends also see, in the leaderboard, how many memories you have planted in the current month — never the content of your private memories.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account remains active. You can delete individual memories, reactions or comments at any time from the relevant screens.

If you delete your account, your data is erased from our systems right away, including memory photos and your profile photo.

Technical logs (including IP addresses) are kept by the infrastructure provider for a short period that depends on the provider (usually from a few days to a few weeks), for security and diagnostics.

Reports are kept for as long as needed to review them and are deleted together with the reported content or user, or when the account of the person who reported is deleted.

Backups managed by the provider may briefly still contain data you have already deleted, until they are overwritten.

6. Your rights

Within the limits set by the GDPR, you have the right to: access your data; ask for its rectification; ask for its erasure; obtain restriction of processing; object to processing; receive your data in a structured, readable format (portability).

Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time from the app controls or the device settings.

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data yourself, from Profile → Account and email → Delete account. This also removes your memory photos and your profile photo.

For any other request (for example a copy of your data), write to memogarden.info@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it) or your local supervisory authority if you believe the processing of your data infringes the law.

7. Security

Data is protected by database-level access rules (Row Level Security): only you can see your private memories, and passwords are never readable by us in clear text. No system is 100% secure, but we follow industry-standard practices to protect your data.

8. Minors

MemoGarden is for people aged 16 and over. By using the app you declare that you are at least 16.

We do not promote the app to minors and we do not knowingly collect data from people under 16.

If we become aware that an account belongs to a person under 16, we suspend it and delete the related data. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, write to memogarden.info@gmail.com.
