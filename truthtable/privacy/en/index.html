Truth Table privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Truth Table is a logic game for iPhone and Android published by Emsy Rune. This page explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • There is no account to create, and we do not collect your name, email or other personal data.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone and is never sent to us.
  • The app is free thanks to ads from Google AdMob, which uses some device data to show them. In Europe, personalised ads appear only if you consent.

Data controller

Marco Vita, who publishes apps under the name Emsy Rune. Contact: emsyrune@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

The app stores only on your device the cases you solved, your stars, your daily case results, your tutorial progress and your settings (theme and vibration). This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted with Impostazioni → Azzera i progressi (Settings → Reset progress; the game is currently in Italian) or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising: Google AdMob

To show banners and videos the app uses Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for the European Economic Area) and Google LLC. When the app requests an ad, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android), only if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and operating system information (model, language, version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps) and technical data used to measure them and prevent fraud.

Google uses this data to select and show ads, measure their performance and prevent abuse. With your consent it may also be used for personalised ads, together with Google's advertising partners, which are listed in the privacy message under Manage options.

Learn more: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Your consent

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. On first launch Google's privacy message appears, where you can accept, decline or choose in detail. If you decline, the game works exactly the same and you will see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Impostazioni → Privacy e annunci (Settings → Privacy and ads).
  • iPhone. iOS asks whether to allow tracking. You can change this in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Legal bases

Personalised ads and the use of the advertising identifier are based on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalised ads, measurement and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in offering the game for free (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Transfers outside the European Union

Google may process data outside the EU, for example in the United States, with the safeguards provided by the GDPR (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses).

Retention

We do not keep any personal data. Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Data collected by Google is kept for the periods stated in its privacy policy.

Children

Truth Table is not specifically aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 14. If you believe this has happened, please contact us.

Your rights

You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and withdraw your consent. Since we keep no personal data, for advertising data you can contact Google directly; for any question write to emsyrune@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Changes

If the way the app uses data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
